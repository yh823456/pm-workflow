# Problem Brief: Customer Review Preparation

## Request

- Requester: Customer Success Lead
- Date: 2026-11-18
- Original wording: "Can product add a bulk export? Preparing customer review packets takes forever."
- Requested solution: Bulk export

## Problem

- Affected users: Customer success managers and product managers supporting strategic accounts
- Scenario: Preparing quarterly and escalation review packets from customer feedback
- Initial problem statement: Review owners manually assemble feedback across multiple accounts because they cannot preserve a filtered view in a shareable format.
- Frequency: Multiple accounts every month; concentrated around quarterly reviews
- Current workaround: Screenshots, copied rows, and hand-built spreadsheets
- User impact: Repetitive work, inconsistent packets, and risk of missing important feedback
- Business impact hypothesis: More preparation time and avoidable errors reduce the time available for interpretation and customer follow-up

## Desired Outcome

The review owner can create a complete, consistent evidence packet for a defined account and time range without manually copying each item.

## Constraints

- Deadline or commitment: No external commitment has been made
- Technical or data constraint: Feedback can contain personal and commercially sensitive information
- Legal, security, or operational constraint: Export must respect account membership, permission, and deletion state

## Evidence Gaps

1. How much time is actually spent preparing one packet?
2. Which feedback fields and filters are required for a useful packet?
3. Is the problem export, report formatting, saved views, or scheduled delivery?
4. Are customers or internal reviewers the primary audience for the packet?

## Intake Decision

- Ready for evidence review: Yes
- Reason: The underlying workflow and impact are specific enough to investigate
- Next action: Complete the evidence log and look for contradictory signals
