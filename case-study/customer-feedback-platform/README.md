# Customer Feedback Platform Case

> **SIMULATED CASE:** The company, users, interviews, quotes, and data are fictional. This material is for workflow practice only and is **NOT EXTERNALLY VALIDATED**.

## Scenario

Harborline Systems is a fictional B2B SaaS company with 160 employees. Its product, Signal Dock, helps product teams collect customer feedback, connect it to product decisions, and track what happened after a decision.

The customer success lead asks the product manager to add bulk export because quarterly business review preparation takes too long.

The product manager must decide whether this is a real problem, whether export is the right solution, and how to define the smallest testable response.

## What This Case Demonstrates

- Separating the requested solution from the underlying problem.
- Mixing simulated interviews with visible assumptions and contradictions.
- Comparing three options instead of accepting the first request.
- Turning a decision into testable scope and acceptance criteria.
- Defining a result review without inventing outcomes.

## Evidence Boundary

The interviews are role-play simulations. They can test the quality of the workflow and expose missing questions, but they cannot prove customer demand.

The next validation step would be to replace each simulated interview with a real interview and record differences from the simulated assumptions.
