## Simulated Interview Notes

### SIMULATED: Customer Success Manager

> "I filter to the customer and quarter, then copy the strongest items into a slide. The filter is useful, but the final packet still needs a summary."

Observed need: preserve the filtered set.

Contradiction: the respondent still expects manual interpretation and formatting after export.

### SIMULATED: Product Manager

> "If I download everything, the packet is too noisy. I need the account filter, date range, and a way to exclude internal notes."

Observed need: export must respect permissions and internal-only fields.

Contradiction: an unrestricted bulk export could increase risk and reduce usefulness.

## Summary

- Strongest evidence: account and date filters are central to the preparation workflow.
- Weakest evidence: time savings from CSV export.
- Largest evidence gap: actual preparation time and the format required by review recipients.
- Contradictory signal: users may need a formatted or scheduled report more than a raw export.
- What would change our mind: repeated real observations showing reviewers need a polished PDF or automatic delivery instead of rows.

- External validation status: Not externally validated

- Known bias: All quote evidence was generated from the same case assumptions
